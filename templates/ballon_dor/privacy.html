Privacy

FansAward is an independent fan project built by one person. This page says exactly what it stores and why.

What we collect when you vote

  • Your top 3 picks. That is the vote.
  • Your email address. Used only to send one confirmation link and to make sure each email votes once. Nothing else is ever sent to it.
  • Your country (optional). Used only for the "votes from X countries" figure.
  • The time of your vote.

What we do not collect

  • No name, no password, no account.
  • The app does not store your IP address.

How long we keep it

  • Votes that are never confirmed by email are deleted after 7 days.
  • When voting closes, emails are replaced with an irreversible anonymous code. The vote counts stay, the email does not.

Things outside our control

  • Our hosting provider (Railway) may keep standard server logs, which can include IP addresses.
  • This site uses Google Analytics to count visits. It sets cookies in your browser. You can block it with your browser settings or an ad blocker.
  • Player photos are served by Cloudinary.

We never

  • sell, share or publish your email,
  • send marketing messages.

Questions or want your vote removed? Email contact@fansaward.com from the address you voted with.
